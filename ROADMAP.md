# Roadmap

Items are intentionally kept small and prioritized by user impact. Low-priority ideas are exploratory and may wait until the core reader needs are covered.

## Very low priority

- **Inertial touchpad scrolling:** after a continuous touchpad gesture ends, continue scrolling with gradually decreasing velocity, similar to a web browser. Check which gesture phases GPUI exposes on the supported Linux/Wayland setups and tune behavior against real touchpads. Keep discrete mouse-wheel steps and keyboard scrolling predictable.
